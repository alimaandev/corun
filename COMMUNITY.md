# 🌍 Corun Community

Welcome to the Corun community! Here's how to connect, share, and contribute.

## 💬 Discussions

- **GitHub Discussions** — [Join here](https://github.com/alimaandev/corun/discussions)
  - 💡 Ideas & feature requests
  - ❓ Questions & help
  - 🎨 Show & tell (share your custom puzzles!)
  - 📢 Announcements

## 🐛 Issues

- **Bug reports** — [Open an issue](https://github.com/alimaandev/corun/issues/new?labels=bug&template=bug_report.yml)
- **Feature requests** — [Open an issue](https://github.com/alimaandev/corun/issues/new?labels=enhancement&template=feature_request.yml)

## 🤝 Contributing

See [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) for the full guide. Quick links:

- [Good first issues](https://github.com/alimaandev/corun/labels/good%20first%20issue) — beginner-friendly
- [Help wanted](https://github.com/alimaandev/corun/labels/help%20wanted) — needs maintainer input
- [PR template](.github/PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md)

## 🧩 Custom Puzzles

Share your creations! When you create a puzzle in the built-in editor, you can share it via a base64 URL. Post your puzzles in [Discussions](https://github.com/alimaandev/corun/discussions/categories/show-and-tell).

## 🌐 Translations

Corun supports English, Spanish, and French. Want to add a new locale? See the [i18n guide](src/lib/i18n.ts) or open an issue.
